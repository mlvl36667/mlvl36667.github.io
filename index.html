Bence Ladóczki

Assistant Research Fellow · BME TMIT

I conduct research in blockchain engineering and cryptographic protocols at BME University, Hungary. I also contribute to quantum chemistry software and build a CCSD(T)-F12 module for MRCC. As a Ph.D. student I worked on the Model Space Quantum Monte Carlo driver of the GELLAN program. I speak Magyar, English, 日本語, 中文 and Русский.

Research Interests

  • GPU/CUDA implementation of BLS signatures
  • Atomic swaps, ECDSA, Schnorr and BLS signatures
  • Elliptic Curve Cryptography & Pairing-Based Cryptography
  • Lightning Network and Layer 2 scaling
  • CCSD(T)-F12 methods for quantum chemistry
  • Monte Carlo methods, MSQMC, FCI-QMC
