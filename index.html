Bence Ladóczki

Associate Professor · Department of Telecommunications and Artificial Intelligence, Budapest University of Technology and Economics, 1111 Budapest, Hungary

I conduct research in blockchain engineering and cryptographic protocols at BME University, Hungary, where I am an Associate Professor. I also contribute to quantum chemistry software and build a CCSD(T)-F12 module for MRCC. As a Ph.D. student I worked on the Model Space Quantum Monte Carlo driver of the GELLAN program. I speak Magyar, English, 日本語, 中文 and Русский.

Research Interests

  • MEV (Maximal Extractable Value) and CEX-DEX arbitrage on Ethereum
  • Ethereum consensus security, single-slot finality, RANDAO
  • Payment channel networks, Lightning Network, cross-chain atomic swaps
  • Machine learning for blockchain (MEV-Boost auctions, video streaming)
  • Explicitly correlated quantum chemistry: CCSD(T)-F12, MP2-F12
  • Quantum Monte Carlo methods (MSQMC, FCI-QMC)
